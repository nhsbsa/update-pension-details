Change your address with your employer

You need to give your employer your new address if you do not have ESR.

How to change your address

  • Give your employer your new address. They can advise you how to do that.
  • Your employer will update the record on the relevant systems.
  • They will notify NHS Pensions that your address has been changed.

You do not need to do anything else after giving your employer the new address.

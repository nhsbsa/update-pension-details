Changing other personal details

This service is to change your address only. How you update other personal details like your name or phone number depends on your member type.

I am currently paying into the scheme

If you're a current NHS employee paying into the scheme (active member) you can update your phone number and email address in the Electronic Staff Record (ESR) if your organisation uses it.

You cannot update your name directly in ESR.

If you do not have ESR, or need to update your name, contact your employer.

I am no longer paying into the scheme or I am retired

Contact us with the personal details you need to update if you are no longer paying into the scheme (deferred member) or are retired. We will update your details for you.

Change your address with NHS Pensions

Use this service to change your address with NHS Pensions and the NHS Pension Scheme.

Who can use this service

You can use this service if you're a member of the NHS Pension Scheme.

Information:

We may contact you if we need more information.

Before you start

You'll need your:

  • NHS membership number or National Insurance number
  • name
  • previous address
  • new address
  • phone number
  • email address

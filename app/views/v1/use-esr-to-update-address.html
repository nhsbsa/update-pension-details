Change your address in ESR

If your NHS organisation uses ESR you can change your address there.

How to change your address

  • Go to the ESR homepage and select 'My Personal Information.
  • From there, select 'Personal Information'.
  • Go to the 'Addresses' section and select 'Update'.
  • Select 'Enter a new address if you have moved' then 'Next'.
  • Follow the remaining steps to complete.

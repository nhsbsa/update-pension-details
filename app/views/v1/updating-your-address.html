Updating your home address

To update your address on your NHS Pensions record you need to provide us with your:

  • previous home's full address
  • new home's full address

We'll start by asking for your previous address. This is the place you are moving away from.

Address on my Total Reward Statement (TRS) is not right

If your address does not look right on your TRS statement, this could be because it was changed after May (cut off) of this year? TRS Statements are updated once a year therefore your address may reflect an old address. Your new address will update on your next TRS statement.
